Site color system

Vice Night

A quiet, neutral base with Miami Vice teal and pink used as accents. Teal does the working jobs (links, focus, status). Pink appears in a few small places. The two meet in a thin teal-to-pink stripe that recalls the Heat Vice jersey trim.

Palette

Each accent has a text-safe shade and a bright shade. Use the bright shades only for fills, stripes, and dots, never for small text on the light background.

Proportion

This keeps it from tipping into costume. If a screen has more pink than teal, pull the pink back.

Rules

  • Teal for links, focus rings, status dots, active states.
  • Pink for one highlight per screen: a "new" tag, the active nav underline, the wordmark dot.
  • The gradient appears only as a 2–3px stripe or on one short headline phrase.
  • Big surfaces stay neutral. Buttons stay ink.
  • No full gradient backgrounds, neon glows, or palm-tree imagery.
  • No pink body text and no bright teal text on the light background.
